Activity 07 · Before you ask

Questions we get first

Anything else, just ask on the scoping call.

Can you work white-label for our agency?

Yes. We work under your agency's name, inside your tools and process. Your client never needs to know a partner was involved.

Which time zones do you cover?

Australian business hours are our home base. We also overlap with UK/Europe and US mornings, and send a written update at the end of your day.

How do you handle client data and NDAs?

We sign your NDA, work inside your Salesforce org or sandbox, follow your access rules, and keep client data out of public AI tools.

How is pricing worked out?

Fixed price per project or a monthly block of hours. You get a one-page scope and price before work starts, so there are no surprise invoices.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for first drafts of code and copy. Every build is then reviewed and tested by hand before it reaches you. AI makes us faster; it doesn't do the QA.

Can we start small?

Please do. A paid test task, such as one template or one automation, is the easiest way to judge the work before committing to more.
