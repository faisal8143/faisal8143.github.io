A journey you can step through, one contact at a time

This is an enrolment nurture journey as it would be built in Journey Builder: a Data Extension entry source, waits, an engagement split, a decision split, a Sales Cloud hand-off and a goal. Pick a contact, press step, and watch the canvas light up as each activity evaluates against that person's real behaviour.

The panel underneath records why every decision went the way it did — including the one most people get wrong, which is that goal and exit criteria are live during a wait, not checked at the end.

What this journey demonstrates

Design decisions

  • Goal and exit are continuous. Priya applies on day 6, inside the five-day wait, and leaves the journey there. She never receives the deadline reminder, because sending it would be nagging someone who already converted.
  • Engagement windows are finite. Tom opens on day 5, but the split evaluated on day 3. An open outside the window does not retroactively move a contact onto the engaged path.
  • Decision splits read the attribute now. Mei opens the first email but her score is 18, so she goes warm. The split reads EngagementScore at that moment, not the value she entered with.
  • Unsubscribe cuts everything. Jordan exits mid-wait on day 1. No further activity evaluates.

Built with

  • Data Extension entry source with re-entry disabled while in journey
  • Engagement split on open within a 3-day window
  • Decision split on a SQL-maintained EngagementScore attribute
  • Sales Cloud activity creating a task plus an SMS to the advisor
  • Goal: application submitted · auto-exit after 30 days
