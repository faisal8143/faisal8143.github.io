Pick a subscriber and the AMPscript is parsed and executed in your browser against that person's Data Extension rows — the greeting, the course, the urgency banner, the call to action and the fee all change because the code decided they should.

Nothing is pre-rendered and there is no Marketing Cloud login. The interpreter is 400 lines of dependency-free JavaScript, written to make personalisation logic reviewable before it reaches a send.
